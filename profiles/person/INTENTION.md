# prim.person

A person as a file. Not a contacts app. Not a CRM row trapped in a vendor.

## Why this is a Prim

These are records on people in the world (someone you know), not users of the app, not accounts, not product profiles. Name, how they relate, phones, emails, a last useful note. If that lives only in an agent's memory or a connector cache, it dies with the session. A `prim.person` pack is the store. Default home is ~/Documents/Prims. Tools cite it. Prims Contacts is the adapter that reads and writes these packs there.

## What it is not

- Not a connector (Prims Contacts is the connector)
- Not a remote control
- Not Rolodex-the-agent (that agent may operate these files; it is not the store)
- Not `prim.ocsf` (company / cap table)

## Noun

Spoken: **person**. Cite: `prim.person`. Id: `person`.
